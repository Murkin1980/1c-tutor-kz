## Licensing rule

Do not bulk-copy IFRS Standards into Atlas or product content. The IFRS Foundation states that use/integration of Standards in products and services can require licensing.

Canonical Atlas records should store:
- source ID;
- standard ID;
- source version;
- effective date;
- structured paragraph locator when permitted;
- bounded factual summary;
- Tutor-authored explanation/case;
- last verification date;
- status.

AI-generated interpretation is never a normative source.
