# Curriculum v0.1 — 12 modules / 4 phases

Target: 12–16 weeks, 100–140 estimated learner-hours, to be validated in CP-01B. Each lesson needs outcome, original RU instruction, EN terminology, synthetic inputs, observable result, rubric, common errors, citations, reviewer.

| ID | Phase | Module | Observable assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M01 | I | US bookkeeper role; CPA/accountant boundaries; small-business types | Identify permitted vs escalated work |
| M02 | I | Accounting equation; chart of accounts; debit/credit | Categorize 30 synthetic entries |
| M03 | I | Cash/accrual fundamentals; P&L, balance sheet, cash flows | Explain 3 financial statements |
| M04 | II | QuickBooks Online setup and navigation; customers/vendors | Configure fictional case in approved sandbox |
| M05 | II | Bank feeds, matching, transfers, card charges, reconciliation | Reconcile month with known variance |
| M06 | II | Invoices, customer payments, bills, expenses, AR/AP | Complete sales and purchase cycle |
| M07 | II | Month close, corrections, adjusting entries, reports | Close month with reproducible balances |
| M08 | III | US tax-adjacent records: W-9, 1099, W-2, sales tax, payroll *concepts only* | Flag transactions for accountant review |
| M09 | III | Service/contractor/landscaping/furniture-sector accounting | Map expenses to client business model |
| M10 | III | Cleanup/catch-up: duplicates, missing transactions, audit trail | Restore a synthetic damaged ledger |
| M11 | IV | Professional English; queries, client handoff, communications | Write a secure document request |
| M12 | IV | Engagements, onboarding, service tiers, proposals and portfolio | Deliver a scoped sample client package |

## Prerequisite dependencies
M01→M02→M03→M04→M05/M06→M07→M10; M08 requires a reviewed compliance layer; M12 requires quality gates M07 and M11.

## Completion thresholds (draft)
- 100% safety-critical questions correct.
- All reconciliations balanced after documented timing items.
- No unexplained suspense balances or hidden mismatches.
- Independent review of answer key before learner publication.
- No learner promise of CPA/tax-preparer qualification.

## Phase boundaries
Phase I foundations; Phase II practical accounting operations; Phase III US compliance context and repairs; Phase IV client communication and commercial readiness.

## First single-lesson pilot
Use `cases/green-valley-landscaping.md`, derive source documents and exact mathematical answer key in CP-01B. QBO demo sessions may reset: do not assume persistence or real bank linkage.
