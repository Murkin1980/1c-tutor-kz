# Green Valley Landscaping LLC — pilot training case

**Fictional entity; TRAINING ONLY.** This is a **case outline**, NOT a balanced ledger or validated financial statement.

## Learner brief
US landscape services business, one training month, USD, synthetic customers and vendors. Planned monthly sales of $18,000, two bank accounts, one credit card, up to 45 statement lines and 12 invoices — planning targets only, not audited data. Payroll, tax remittance and real bank linking excluded.

## Workflow
1. Read fictional invoice and expense evidence.
2. Enter customer invoice in external approved demo or synthetic worksheet.
3. Recognize incoming payment without double-counting income.
4. Enter supplier bill and payment.
5. Book synthetic bank service fee.
6. Reconcile month-end cash accounts, documenting outstanding items.
7. Produce P&L and Balance Sheet with an explicit reporting basis.
8. Explain discrepancies in Russian using English software labels.

## CP-01B data deliverables
- Company and chart-of-accounts fixture.
- Invoices, bills and receipts with stable synthetic identifiers.
- Separate bank/credit-card statements plus starting balances.
- Journal/ledger and month-end trial balance.
- Deterministic answer key and reconciliation checklist.
- At least three deliberate learner-error cases and teaching feedback.
- Independent accounting review and date.

**Do not invent a 'PASS' before transactions and expected outputs have been independently checked.**
