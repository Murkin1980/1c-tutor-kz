# International Accountant Capability Research Pass

Date: 2026-08-15
Status: initial market evidence pass
Purpose: derive real professional capabilities, not copy course curricula.

## Executive finding

The market signal strongly favors operational accounting capability over isolated standards knowledge.

Recurring work patterns across current remote/accounting roles include:
- month-end close;
- journal entries;
- accruals and prepaids;
- bank/account reconciliations;
- AP/AR;
- general ledger ownership;
- fixed assets;
- intercompany;
- audit support and working papers;
- financial reporting;
- investigation/resolution of discrepancies;
- strong Excel;
- cloud ERP/accounting systems;
- English communication and remote autonomy.

For IFRS-oriented international roles, IFRS knowledge appears alongside these operational capabilities, not instead of them.

- NetSuite;
- QuickBooks Online/Desktop;

- Sage Intacct;

Tutor implication:

Do not build clones of all these systems. Teach software-independent accounting work products and navigation concepts, then create lightweight transfer modules for selected high-demand platforms if later research justifies them.

## Product implications

1. International Track should start with operational accounting, not a long IFRS lecture block.
2. Bank reconciliation is a strong first international simulation candidate because it is frequent, software-independent and objectively verifiable.
3. Month-end close should become a capstone environment integrating multiple capabilities.
4. IFRS topics should be introduced as treatment decisions inside familiar workflows/cases.
5. The learner needs Excel-like working-paper skills even if Tutor does not embed Microsoft Excel itself.
6. Job readiness should be tested by asynchronous case/work-sample packs with incomplete evidence and reviewer feedback.
7. 1C remains valuable: at least one current CIS international role explicitly combines Kazakhstan 1C, NetSuite, IFRS and reconciliation responsibilities.

## Research limitations

This is an initial market sample, not a statistically representative labor-market dataset.

Next research expansion should deliberately sample:
- UK/EU IFRS roles;
- GCC international accounting/shared services;
- remote outsourcing/bookkeeping providers;
- Big Four / mid-tier outsourced finance roles;
- freelance platforms;
- entry/intermediate roles, not only senior roles;
- actual hiring work samples where publicly described.

Use those results to re-rank capabilities before implementing International Track curriculum.
