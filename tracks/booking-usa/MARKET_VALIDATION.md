# Commercial validation — US small business remote bookkeeping

Status: hypotheses only; no clients, revenues or certification verified.

## Target segments
Prioritize straightforward service companies (cleaning, landscaping, installation) without complex payroll, sales-tax nexus, inventory or construction WIP. Potential differentiation: familiarity with furniture/contractor processes, but pilot complexity should remain low.

## Candidate offers (NOT validated market rates)
- Starter monthly books: candidate $250/month.
- Standard monthly books + AR/AP: candidate $450/month.
- Cleanup/catch-up: quote after paid/limited diagnostic, never a blind fixed fee.
Exclude federal/state tax filing, CPA attestation and regulated advice unless properly authorized.

## Test design (proposed CP-04)
- 10–15 structured interviews with US small-business owners/accounting firms.
- 20 narrow outreach attempts to agencies/firms with no spam automation.
- Compare 3 offers and objections: proof of quality, security, language, time zone, insurance, region, remote workflow.
- Small paid trial with explicit boundaries, client-owned subscriptions and least-privilege access.
- Measure qualified response rate, discovery calls, conversion, effective hourly pay, rework rate and ongoing workload.
- Validate cross-border contracting, invoicing, Kazakhstan tax and US state laws with qualified advisers.

## Go/no-go questions
Are there clients willing to pay? Can staff safely access tools from KZ under terms? Can all work be independently checked? Is gross margin positive after software, review, payment fees and taxes? Can work be delivered without impersonating a US CPA?
