# Topic Bridges — Local Practice to International Accounting

Status: skeleton / source-backed standards mapping, not full curriculum
Date: 2026-08-15

The purpose of a Topic Bridge is to connect skills already learned in 1C/local accounting to deeper accounting logic, IFRS and international work products.

## Fixed Assets

Canonical topic: `topic:fixed-assets`

Layers:
- 1C Practice: acquisition / capitalization workflow / depreciation-related operations when implemented;
- Accounting KZ: local treatment, only when sourced and methodologically verified;
- Accounting Concepts: asset recognition, cost, useful life, residual value, depreciation;
- IFRS: `standard:ias-16`;
- International Practice: fixed asset register, additions/disposals schedule, depreciation roll-forward, reconciliation;
- English: property, plant and equipment (PPE), carrying amount, useful life, residual value, depreciation;
- Assessment: determine capitalizable vs expense items, prepare schedule/entry, explain statement impact.

## Inventory

Canonical topic: `topic:inventory`

Layers:
- 1C Practice: receipt, movement, inventory count, write-off/production links;
- Accounting Concepts: cost, expense recognition, net realisable value;
- IFRS: `standard:ias-2`;
- International Practice: inventory reconciliation, aging/NRV review, cut-off support;
- English: inventory, cost of sales, write-down, net realisable value;
- Assessment: reconcile quantity/value, identify write-down issue, explain P&L/SFP impact.

## Revenue

Canonical topic: `topic:revenue`

Layers:
- 1C Practice: customer invoice, payment, realization;
- Accounting Concepts: revenue is not the same event as cash collection or invoice issuance;
- IFRS: `standard:ifrs-15`;
- International Practice: revenue cut-off, contract review, supporting schedule;
- English: contract, performance obligation, transaction price, revenue recognition;
- Assessment: distinguish invoice/payment/delivery/performance, select timing and statement impact.

## Leases

Canonical topic: `topic:leases`

Layers:
- 1C/local practice: future sourced local workflow where relevant;
- Accounting Concepts: right to use an asset vs recurring cash payment;
- IFRS: `standard:ifrs-16`;
- International Practice: lease schedule, liability roll-forward, interest/depreciation support;
- English: right-of-use asset, lease liability, discount rate, lease payments;
- Assessment: build a simple schedule and explain SFP/P&L effects.

## Cash and Cash Flows

Canonical topic: `topic:cash-and-cash-flows`

Layers:
- 1C Practice: bank receipt/write-off, bank statement, cash operations;
- Accounting Concepts: ledger cash vs bank evidence; reconciliation;
- IFRS: `standard:ias-7` for cash-flow reporting layer;
- International Practice: bank reconciliation and cash-flow classification;
- English: outstanding item, bank charge, deposit in transit, cash flow;
- Assessment: complete bank reconciliation and classify selected cash-flow items.

## Impairment

Canonical topic: `topic:impairment`

Layers:
- 1C/local practice: connect later to asset/inventory/receivable evidence where appropriate;
- Accounting Concepts: carrying amount, recoverability, loss recognition;
- IFRS: `standard:ias-36` for applicable non-financial assets;
- International Practice: impairment indicator review and supporting calculation;
- Assessment: identify indication, calculate simplified impact, document rationale.

## Financial Instruments / Receivables

Canonical topic: `topic:financial-instruments`

Layers:
- 1C Practice: receivable/payable/payment and settlement flows;
- Accounting Concepts: contractual rights/obligations, measurement, credit loss;
- IFRS: `standard:ifrs-9`;
- International Practice: AR aging, expected-credit-loss support, reconciliation;
- English: financial asset, financial liability, receivable, expected credit loss;
- Assessment: reconcile receivable and identify credit-loss issue.

## Cross-cutting operational topics

These may not map one-to-one to a single IFRS Standard but are critical professional capabilities:
- bank reconciliation;
- AP reconciliation;
- AR reconciliation;
- accruals;
- prepayments;
- journal entries;
- trial balance;
- month-end close;
- year-end close;
- supporting schedules;
- audit preparation;
- intercompany reconciliation;
- management reporting;
- consolidation fundamentals.

These topics connect BusinessProcess + Capability + Case + Assessment nodes, with Standard nodes added only where a specific IFRS rule is relevant.

## Rule

Never teach the standard number first if the learner can enter through a business event they already understand.

Preferred bridge:

`known transaction → accounting question → economic substance → standard → work product → assessment`.
